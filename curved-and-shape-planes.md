Draw!3D is a 3D drawing app for Android tablets. Besides flat planes, you can draw on **curved planes** (bending walls you sketch yourself) and **3D shape planes** (spheres, cubes and other solid shapes). This guide shows how to make each one, draw on it, and manage it afterwards.

## What is a curved plane?

A curved plane is a surface that bends along a line you draw. Think of it as a ribbon of paper standing on its edge: you draw the path of the ribbon, and Draw!3D stretches it away from you into a surface you can draw on.

Good for: winding walls, garden borders, paths, hills, waves, rounded towers and curved signs.

## How do I make a curved plane?

1. Choose the view to draw the curve from. The curve is the plane's edge as seen from where you're looking, and the surface stretches straight away from you.
   - From the **Top** view, the curve becomes a standing wall that follows your line, like a fence or a garden border.
   - From the **Front** view, the curve becomes a surface that bends toward and away from you, like a wave or a hill.
2. Open **Tools** and tap **Draw Curved Plane**.
3. Draw your curve in one stroke.
4. Unlock Orbit and turn the camera to see the new surface.

## How do I resize a curved plane?

Right after drawing a curved plane, handles appear on it:

- **Corner handles:** drag one away from the middle of the curve to make the whole plane bigger, or toward the middle to make it smaller.
- **Arrow:** drag it outward to make the surface stretch further (taller, if you drew it from the top view), or inward to make it shorter.

Resizing can be undone with **Undo**.

## Drawing, filling and erasing on a curved plane

- **Draw:** just draw on the surface. Your strokes follow the curve. Whichever side of the plane you're looking at is the side you draw on, so you can decorate both the inside and outside of a curved wall.
- **Fill:** tap inside a closed shape drawn on the curve, like a circle or square, to fill it. The fill follows the curve. Tap a filled shape again with a new color to change it.
- **Erase:** the point eraser cuts gaps in lines where you rub. The whole-line eraser removes any line it touches.
- **Smudge and Blend** work on flat planes only for now.

## What is a 3D shape plane?

A 3D shape plane is a solid shape you can draw directly on, with your strokes wrapping around its surface. The shapes are:

- Sphere
- Cube
- Cylinder
- Cone
- Capsule
- Pyramid

Good for: heads and faces, planets, balls, bushes and trees, dice, barrels, lamp posts, rocks and character bodies.

## How do I place a 3D shape plane?

1. Open **Tools** and tap **Draw 3D Shape Plane**, then choose the shape.
2. Press on the canvas where the shape should go and drag to set its size.
3. Fine-tune the size with the arrows:
   - **Red**, **green** and **blue** arrows stretch the shape in one direction. Use them to make a tall cylinder, a flat cube or an egg-shaped sphere.
   - The **white ball** in the middle makes the whole shape bigger or smaller evenly.
4. Tap **Place**.

Once placed, the shape is **locked**, so it can't be knocked out of position while you draw on it. To move it later, use the move button in the Planes list (see below).

## Drawing and erasing on a 3D shape

- **Draw:** draw on the shape from any side. Unlock Orbit to turn around it and keep drawing on the back, top and sides.
- **Erase:** the point eraser cuts gaps in lines; the whole-line eraser removes lines you touch.
- **Fill, Smudge and Blend** aren't available on 3D shapes yet.

## Managing curved and shape planes

Each curved plane and 3D shape gets its own entry in the **Planes** list (open **Layers**, then the **Planes** tab). Curved planes are listed as **Curved Plane** followed by a number.

- **Go back to it:** tap its name. The camera and paper return to that plane, and the shape keeps the size you gave it.
- **Hide it:** tap the **eye** button to hide the plane and everything drawn on it. Tap again to show it.
- **Move it:** tap the **↕** button and use the sliders. The plane and its drawings move together.
- **Delete it:** tap the **trash** button. This removes the plane, the shape and everything drawn on it. **Undo** brings it back.
- **Draw freely again:** tap **Reset Camera** to stop drawing on that plane and go back to new planes facing the camera.

Curved planes and 3D shapes are saved with your canvas, including everything drawn on them.

## Ideas to try

- **A dice:** place a **Cube** and draw dots on each face.
- **A planet:** place a **Sphere**, draw continents, and add a ring with a **Curved Plane** drawn around it.
- **A garden:** from the **Top** view, use **Draw Curved Plane** for a flower bed edge, draw flowers on it, then add **Sphere** bushes.
- **A tower:** place a tall **Cylinder** with a **Cone** roof and draw windows and bricks on them.
- **A character:** use a **Sphere** for the head and a **Capsule** for the body.

## Common questions

### Why can't I draw on my curved plane?

Make sure the plane is selected: tap it in the Planes list. If you tapped **Reset Camera**, new strokes go to new flat planes instead.

### Which side of a curved plane will I draw on?

The side you're looking at. Turn the camera to the other side to draw there.

### Can I change a 3D shape's size after placing it?

Not yet. Shapes lock when you tap Place. To change one, delete it in the Planes list and place a new one.

### Why doesn't Fill work on my sphere?

Fill is available on flat and curved planes, but not on 3D shapes yet. Use a brush to color the shape instead.

### Can I move a shape or curved plane after drawing on it?

Yes. Use the **↕** move button in the Planes list. Everything drawn on it moves with it.
